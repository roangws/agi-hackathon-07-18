# Demo video — who does what

**You:** start QuickTime (⌘⇧5, Record Entire Screen), read each line when its cue
appears, stop recording at the end. Nothing else.
**Claude:** resets the mesh and drives every click, tab switch, kill, reload, and
chat message in the browser, paced to this script. Say "go" when recording.

Keep the browser full screen before saying go.

## Shots

**Shot 1 (0:00). CUE: page loads, orbs appear, feed starts filling with agent messages.**
Read: "80% of serious medical errors trace back to patient handoffs between staff.
Work gets dropped at shift change. This is AGI Hospital, a hospital operations
center run by 8 live agents."

**Shot 2 (~0:15). CUE: right panel switches to the Audit Log tab for a few seconds, then back to Channels.**
Read: "Here is the idea. The design comes from Cotal, a Slack style mesh for
agents. Every message and every step of work is written to a permanent log in
InsForge Postgres before the work moves on. One log gives you 2 things. Agents
that survive crashes, and the audit trail hospitals must keep."

**Shot 3 (~0:30). CUE: right panel switches to the Census tab. You will see patient rows, colored progress bars, and at least one row with a chain icon "⛓ waiting on".**
Read: "The work is genuine. Each step is Claude, writing discharge summaries and
medication checks on synthetic patients. Tasks chain like care does. Transport
waits for a bed. Finish a task and the agent messages the next specialist."

**Shot 4 (~0:45). CUE: panel returns to Channels and a highlighted message appears: "sent SIGKILL to <agent>'s worker process (pid …)". One orb's label will turn grey/TERMINATED about 30 seconds later.**
Read slowly across the wait: "Now the moment this was built for. I kill an agent
in the middle of a discharge. Its process ends on my machine. The mesh notices
the silence."
**CUE: big center toast "HANDOFF NEVER DROPPED" with a bookmark number.**
Read: "The other agents race for the orphaned task, the database picks exactly
one winner, and it resumes from the exact step in the log where its colleague
stopped. Every step runs once. The patient stays covered. We ship the tests that
prove both claims."

**Shot 5 (~1:15). CUE: look at the five small chips at the bottom right of the graph (handoffs done, rescues, avg rescue, steps repeated, handoffs dropped). Then the page reloads by itself and the feed refills.**

rescues, zero steps repeated, zero dropped. Reload and the whole history rebuilds
from InsForge. That is the audit trail in action."

**Shot 6 (~1:30). CUE: red ED SURGE message in the feed, then a "RunType agent:" reply line. After that, a chat message is typed into the bottom composer and Atlas answers.**

can talk to any agent. The charge nurse answers from the live census."

**Shot 7 (~1:45). CUE: quiet wide view of the whole deck, agents working.**
Read: "Hospitals already pay for handoff safety and for audit trails. This gives
them both from one system, priced per facility, and the same engine fits any

**Shot 8 (~2:00). CUE: same wide view, nothing new happens.**
Read: "The agent dies. The work continues. The log proves it. AGI Hospital,
